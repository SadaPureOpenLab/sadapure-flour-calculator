# Books and articles reviewed — scope of access

The research reviewed accessible descriptions, abstracts, selected sections and available previews. It is not a full systematic review or an exhaustive search of every book worldwide. University website searching was stopped after the user's preference; subsequent work uses publishers, Google Books, journal articles and public food references.

## Books

- [Cereal Grains: Properties, Processing, and Nutritional Attributes](https://books.google.com/books/about/Cereal_Grains.html?id=OgzSBQAAQBAJ), Sergio O. Serna-Saldivar, CRC Press. Bibliographic record, description and available contents checked; full book not reviewed.
- [Flour and Breads and Their Fortification in Health and Disease Prevention](https://books.google.com/books/about/Flour_and_Breads_and_Their_Fortification.html?id=vG-KDwAAQBAJ), Academic Press, second edition. Available description/preview checked; not evidence of an exact condition-specific roti blend.
- [Nuts and Seeds in Health and Disease Prevention](https://books.google.com/books/about/Nuts_and_Seeds_in_Health_and_Disease_Pre.html?id=deXEDwAAQBAJ), Academic Press. Bibliographic/preview access only; individual full chapters not reviewed.
- [Sorghum and millets in human nutrition](https://www.fao.org/4/T0818E/T0818E00.htm), FAO Food and Nutrition Series. Available contents and nutrition/antinutrient sections reviewed. Varieties and processing affect composition and bioavailability.

## Articles and food references

- [Oats and beta-glucan trials](https://drc.bmj.com/content/10/5/e002784): evidence concerns specific trial preparations and doses. It does not validate the app's flour percentages.
- [Oilseed enrichment in breads](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6262637/): processing form and inclusion level affect bakery characteristics; roti suitability is not established automatically.
- [Pulse and wheat flour blending](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9601813/): technical formulation evidence does not imply a clinical prescription.
- [Amaranth, quinoa and buckwheat wholemeal flour study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8003493/): results concern tested samples, not unmeasured supplier flours.
- [FAO pulse reference](https://www.fao.org/fileadmin/user_upload/pulses-2016/docs/factsheets/Health_EN_PRINT.pdf): pulses and preparation; phytate can affect mineral availability, so iron content is not an absorbed-dose calculation.
- [Teff food applications](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4571201/): food application review; no therapeutic flour protocol inferred.
- [International guideline article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10505534/): no superior diet composition established for the reviewed condition; no universal flour percentages supplied.
- [Sport nutrition position stand](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10210857/): energy and nutrients depend on context; sex alone is not an exact flour formula.
- [FDA raw flour safety](https://www.fda.gov/consumers/consumer-updates/flour-raw-food-and-other-safety-facts), [allergens](https://www.fda.gov/food/nutrition-food-labeling-and-critical-foods/food-allergies), [lupin](https://www.fda.gov/food/food-additives-petitions/lupin-and-allergenicity-frequently-asked-questions).
- [NCCIH fenugreek](https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/fenugreek): limited condition evidence and relevant safety limitations; extract studies are not flour-dose instructions.

## Accepted conclusions

Food-family roles can inform ingredient exploration. Exact published brand ratios can be transcribed with provenance and arithmetic checks. Experimental percentages must be labelled as software choices. Unknown ingredients, missing percentages, food forms, clinical benefit, nutrient composition and cooking suitability must remain explicit limitations.

Model training is not an accessible, verified source database and is not cited as evidence. No claims of having read complete unavailable books are made.
