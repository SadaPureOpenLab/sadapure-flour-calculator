## Source checks

Diabetes, stamina and hair/skin brand rows total 100%. The PCOS page lists 97% / 4,850 g under a 5 kg heading. The app explicitly rescales all existing rows; the missing 3% is not invented. The resulting example is custom and source-adjusted. Hair and skin share the same published source recipe, so their unmodified ratios are identical; no artificial difference is invented solely to produce a different number.

## Goal and focus behaviour

Generate uses both selected goal and selected focus. Goal changes hide the previous table until regeneration. A goal-default example can be combined equally with one of four culinary focuses; all adjustments and exclusions are displayed. Both source and adjusted status travel with local CSV/PDF exports. Shared URLs contain only quantity and percentages, not concern names or private text.

## Demographics

Gender, age and body weight alone do not establish a flour composition. Nutrition needs can differ, but this arithmetic tool has no validated individualized nutrient model. Source marketing names do not constitute evidence for a male/female therapeutic ratio. For a reviewed individual food plan, add actual nutrient mapping, serving-size assumptions and qualified dietetic review first.
