Before adding condition-specific proportions, obtain qualified dietetic review and documented evidence. Before adding nutrient values, map food form and food ID, units and serving assumptions. Before describing a mixture as gluten-free or safe for allergy, review actual sourcing, labelling and manufacturing controls. Do not infer certification or individual safety from the UI.
